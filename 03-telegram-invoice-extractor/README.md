# AI Telegram Invoice Extractor

![Telegram Invoice Extractor Workflow](../assets/telegram-invoice-extractor-workflow.png)

A Make.com automation that receives invoice files through Telegram, extracts key invoice data with AI, converts it into structured information, and stores the result automatically.

## Business Problem

Finance and operations teams often spend time manually reading invoices and entering invoice details into spreadsheets or internal systems. This process is repetitive and can lead to data-entry errors.

## Solution

This automation allows users to send an invoice through Telegram. The workflow downloads the file, extracts important invoice fields with AI, converts the information into structured data, stores the result in Google Sheets, and sends a confirmation back to the user.

## Workflow

1. Receive an invoice through Telegram
2. Download the invoice file
3. Extract invoice information with AI
4. Convert the extracted result into structured data
5. Add the invoice data to Google Sheets
6. Send a confirmation or result back through Telegram
